What This Is

A weekly training built on one idea: your business already has the logic to grow.

Most programs bring a framework and apply it. Cracking the Code works differently. We find the logic that's already working inside your business — the patterns, the strengths, the hidden leverage — and we show you how to use it deliberately.

Every Monday, business owners gather to work on real problems: conversion, retention, revenue structure, team alignment, profit clarity. Not theory. Implementation.

What the Room Is Doing This Month

Is it IP yet? November is spent finding out.

Most owners already own more intellectual property than they have ever written down. All November, the room finds it, names it, writes it down so it belongs to the business, and carries it out to the partners who represent you. An advantage written down is property a buyer pays for. An advantage left unwritten is a habit that leaves with the person who has it.

  • 2 November — Property Worth Protecting. What your business has, what it owns, and what it actually protects.
  • 9 November — End Zones and Naming Your Method. Giving the way you get clients results a name of its own.
  • 16 November — IP with Integrating Preeminence. The room went to school on Amazon reviews and rewrote each owner's message in the market's own words.
  • 23 November — The Magic Mirror. Seeing each client more clearly than they see themselves, and speaking to each one in their own words.
  • 30 November — A fifth-Monday special. Turning November's IP into a system, and the bridge into December's Success Systems & Profits.

Try it before you join: the IP Audit scores what your business has, owns and protects, and where your preeminence outperforms your closest competitor. Brian and Scott read every audit sent to them before Monday.

Format

Weekly live sessions, Mondays at 9AM. Structured around a real business problem. You leave with something you can use the same week.

Looking Back

Three themes the room has already worked through.

Every session leaves a working page behind. Here are three, with what each covered and what owners took home from it.

September 2026 · Absolute Advantage

How You Are Heard, Seen and Trusted

How a client knows you listened, how your voice and your difference land before your first point, and how trust speeds every deal.

Takeaways: say the client's situation back to them better than they said it, and listen for "that's right," not "you're right." Then send the recap in writing, because it proves you listened a second time.

October 2026 · Enhancing Exits

Position Your Business for a Win in 80 Days

One deal in eighty days: what the business needs, three real options for getting it, and the partner who already holds the missing piece.

Takeaways: put three options on the table and one honest objection against your favorite. Owners who weigh two or more options fail far less often than those deciding yes or no on one. Then look before, during, after and instead of your sale for your partners.

October 2026 · Enhancing Exits

Leading YOUR Group

Bringing together the people your business serves, so you become the person they all trust and the one they refer. A nineteen-part course and a one-page checklist.

Takeaways: write the structure before anyone is invited: one sentence on who it is for, three agreements said aloud, a fixed day and hour. Then test it with ten people you know, and ask members for referrals, never for a sale.
